About
ARGOS

ARGOS is an investing platform that integrates financial education into the investment experience. Through market insights, company information and accessible learning tools, it helps people understand the market, evaluate opportunities and make informed decisions with confidence.

The name is inspired by the Argo, the ship that carried the Argonauts through unfamiliar waters in Greek mythology. Navigating the seas takes an understanding of changing conditions, awareness of uncertainty and a sense of direction. Investing takes the same: knowledge, perspective and thoughtful decision-making.

Purpose,
mission,
vision

Our purpose

We believe investing should begin with understanding rather than pressure or speculation. We exist to make investing more approachable by helping people understand financial markets, recognize potential risks, and develop independent judgment.

Our mission

Integrate financial education into every stage of the investing experience, making learning a natural part of the decision-making process. We help investors evaluate opportunities, understand risks, and make decisions with greater confidence and intention.

Our vision

A healthier investing culture where knowledge guides decisions, curiosity encourages exploration, and confidence comes from understanding. A future where investors navigate changing markets, think independently, and decide in line with their own goals and values.

Our
values

Understanding

We make complex financial information accessible so investors can understand the companies, markets and economic conditions behind their decisions.

Independence

We empower investors to develop their own judgment rather than simply follow others.

Responsibility

We communicate financial information, potential opportunities and investment risks with clarity, transparency and honesty.

Exploration

We encourage curiosity and continuous learning as investors navigate changing markets.

In practice: make complex information easier to understand, encourage independent thinking, communicate risk transparently, and create chances to keep learning throughout the investing experience.

Market
context

Investing offers opportunities for financial growth, but navigating markets is hard for beginners. Complex terminology, shifting conditions and conflicting opinions make it difficult to evaluate information and understand risk.

That calls for an experience that connects financial knowledge with practical decision-making: helping users understand not only how to invest, but why they make certain choices.

audience

20s – early 30sBeginner investorsUnited States

Young adults who are interested in investing but have limited financial knowledge. They want a deeper understanding of the companies they consider, upcoming financial events, and the economic forces shaping the market, so they can assess opportunities from several angles and build their own strategy.

Brand
positioning

An information-driven investing platform for emerging investors who want more clarity in financial decision-making.

Company research, earnings announcements, market insights and financial education are integrated into one investing experience, so investors can assess opportunities, weigh risks and decide their next step on their own judgment.

Brand
personality

ClearInformedObjectiveConfident

An analytical character establishes credibility; a structured, straightforward approach makes complex subjects easier to navigate. Every expression of the brand should convey precision, transparency and a strong sense of direction: knowledgeable, composed and dependable.

Brand
voice

Direct, precise and grounded in facts.

Present financial information with enough context that readers can tell confirmed details apart from interpretation and possible outcomes. Speak with authority without overstating certainty. Every message stays informative, measured, and respectful of the reader’s ability to reach their own conclusions.

Voice
principles

Be direct

Lead with the most relevant information.

Be precise

Use specific facts and explain their significance.

Be balanced

Present opportunities alongside relevant risks.

Be measured

Avoid speculation, exaggeration and artificial urgency.

Examples

✓“The Federal Reserve has raised interest rates by 0.25 percentage points. Here’s how the change may affect different sectors.”
✕“Big changes are happening in the economy! Find out what everyone is talking about!”
✓“The company has reported strong revenue growth, although rising operating costs may affect future profitability.”
✕“With revenue growing this fast, the company is clearly heading toward success!”

Visual

Logo

The logo reflects direction, stability and independent navigation.

The symbol combines the letter A with the form of a ship: exploration, and the freedom to chart one’s own course. Its 60° triangular structure conveys precision, balance and direction, reflecting ARGOS’s analytical and confident character.

Logo
grid

The logo follows a proportional system based on the uppercase A of the wordmark. The cap height of A is the unit: the gap between symbol and wordmark is ½ A, a consistent 1 : 2 relationship in the vertical lockup.

Logo
lockup

The symbol and wordmark may be used independently, but must never be combined in a horizontal lockup.

Logo
color

The logo is permitted in exactly two combinations. No others.

Logo
misuse

Always use the logo in its approved configuration. Keep its original proportions, spacing and colors in every application. Do not distort it, resize individual elements, apply unauthorized colors, or rearrange the symbol and wordmark.

forms

The logo extends into a dynamic system: the ship form is isolated from the symbol and waves are introduced beneath it. The waves respond to real market data, changing in form and intensity across four levels in 25% increments, connecting the identity to market activity.

Main
colors

Navigator Purple is the signature color: confidence, clarity and direction. Cloud White is the primary background, a clean environment that keeps information in focus. Click any value to copy it.

Support
colors

Supporting colors set hierarchy and structure. Focus Purple carries headlines and section titles, Deep Horizon is the main text color, and the three grays support interface elements, subtle backgrounds, dividers and secondary information.

Accent
colors

Used selectively in charts, icons and illustrations to highlight key information and communicate market conditions. Never used for typography, with one exception: Signal Mint headlines on Navigator Purple.

Market
semantics

Signal Mint = up
Upward movement and positive trends
Sunset Orange = down / risk
Downward movement, warnings, risk indicators
Mint on white is below 2:1 contrast. On light surfaces, pair mint lines with a label or arrow so meaning never depends on color alone.

Approved
pairings

Text and background combinations allowed by the type & color rules, with live WCAG contrast. AA needs 4.5:1 for body text and 3:1 for large headlines.

Graphic
motif

    

      
The Market Tides motif

      
Derived from the 60° geometry of the logo, Market Tides translates market movement into a structured wave pattern. The points where waves converge follow the logo’s defining angle; rhythm and amplitude express levels of market activity and volatility.

      

      
Repeat and connect motifs at consistent intervals to build a continuous pattern. Keep spacing even and preserve the original 60° structure. Use across business cards, wall graphics, packaging and environmental design.

Icons

    

      
Icon style & construction

      
Simple geometric line icons with a clean, minimal look. Every icon uses a consistent 2.5 pt stroke and the 60° directional angle from the logo. New icons follow the same stroke weight, geometric logic and angle-based construction.

      

      
Construction view only. Purple marks the 60° directional elements; gray marks the base shapes. In use, every icon is a single color.

    

  

  

    
Icon
color
usage

    

      
Icons are always one color. Navigator Purple icons sit on Cloud White or light backgrounds. Signal Mint, Cloud White and Echo Purple may be used on dark backgrounds. Sunset Orange is reserved for negative indicators, warnings and risk.

Image

    

      

        
Photography direction

        
Clean, natural and trustworthy. Predominantly white or light backgrounds, with purple accents through clothing, objects or environmental details.

        
Subtle angles and dynamic perspectives add depth and movement while compositions stay balanced and natural. People, objects and environments should feel authentic and effortless.

      

      

        
        
      

    

  

  

    
Image
don’ts

    

      

        
Avoid outdated corporate imagery, overly staged stock photography, and visuals disconnected from the brand.

        
Don’t use backgrounds dominated by colors outside the brand palette, generic nature imagery, or exaggerated expressions. Avoid artificial styling and excessive retouching.

Quick
rules

    

      
Logo
Vertical lockup is primary. Symbol-only for icons and avatars; wordmark-only for narrow headers. Never horizontal. Purple on white or white on purple only.

      
Color
Navigator Purple + Cloud White lead. Mint means up, Orange means down or risk. Accents never set type, except Mint headlines on Navigator Purple.

      
Type
Elza only. Medium for display, Semibold caps for labels, Regular for body. Sentence case by default.

      
Voice
Direct, precise, balanced, measured. Pair every opportunity with its risk. No hype, no urgency, no certainty you can’t back up.
